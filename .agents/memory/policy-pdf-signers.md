---
name: Policy PDF signers
description: The Política SST report must reflect the signers selected and stored on that policy.
---

The Política SST PDF uses the policy's saved signer IDs and names for ELABORÓ, AUTORIZÓ and APROBÓ. It must not replace them with the company's generic standard signers.

**Why:** The policy form allows selecting approval participants, but the generic PDF footer previously ignored those selections, so the generated report did not represent the created policy.

**How to apply:** When changing the Política SST form or PDF, keep the three policy signer fields synchronized end-to-end: searchable selection in the form, persisted worker IDs, and the same names/roles in the report and signature footer.
